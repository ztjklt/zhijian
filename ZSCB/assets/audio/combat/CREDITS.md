# Combat audio sources

All newly downloaded source takes below are offered as CC0 1.0. Selected originals are in `sources/` (not imported by Godot). `manifest.json` maps every playable asset to its exact source. `tools/build_combat_audio.py` records trimming, mono 44.1kHz conversion, short fades, normalization, layering and original procedural transients. These are game vocals; processed creature sounds for rabbit/deer/bat are stylized, not claimed as field recordings of those species.

- **StarNinjas** — [20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)](https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes), CC0. Knife-based blade takes and clashes, selected 1/3/5 and 1/2/3.
- **rubberduck** — [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx), CC0. Selected blade, wood and stone takes 01–03.
- **HaelDB** — [Male Grunt/Yelling sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds), CC0 option. Selected 3yell1, 3yell3, 2yell4, distinct performed takes.
- **Nocturnal_Vanguard / AuraVoice** — [Female Scream 1](https://opengameart.org/content/female-scream-1), CC0. One take, three subtly processed variants.
- **rubberduck** — [80 CC0 creature SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx), CC0. Selected screams, hurt, bug and roar takes, repitched for game species.
- **SFXMint** — [Horse Neigh](https://sfxmint.com/sounds/horse-neigh), CC0. horse-neigh-01/02/03. Provider describes its collection as AI-generated or procedurally synthesized and individually checked; these are not claimed as natural field recordings.
