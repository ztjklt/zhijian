Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one full-body combat-idle character design reference for a side-scrolling 2D stickman action game.
Primary request: Design a minimalist stickman whose entire body clearly communicates a calm, alert, battle-ready guard. The pose is the main subject. Keep the existing visual identity: charcoal-black circular head, narrow white angular eyes, a slim line-like trunk and long simple limbs, smooth clean silhouette.

View: fixed side-scroller camera, character facing screen right. Subtle three-quarter attitude in the head and shoulders, without strong perspective or foreshortening. One complete character only; all hands and feet fully visible.

Pose mechanics: a balanced, slightly lowered fighting guard. Feet staggered front-to-back with a moderate gap, the lead foot forward and grounded and the rear heel lightly raised with the ball of the rear foot touching the same ground level. Both knees softly bent. Hips lowered slightly, body balanced between the two feet. Torso leans slightly toward the opponent; head subtly tucked while eyes look forward toward screen right. Both elbows bent and kept fairly close to the trunk. Lead fist slightly forward near mouth height; rear fist near the lower cheek and chin. The fists are at different depths and slightly different heights. Shoulders and hips have a subtle opposing turn. All limb joints bend coherently. Convey stored energy and readiness to strike, block or evade, rather than a completed attack.

Silhouette: preserve clear small gaps between both fists and the head, between forearms and torso, and between the two legs. The guard must read clearly even with the eyes hidden and at small game scale. Avoid overlapping arms becoming one black lump. Elegant asymmetry, one clear forward line of action, secure visible foot contacts. No hanging hands, straight locked knees, parallel close feet, upright parade stance, extreme wide squat, raised victory arms or casual walking pose.

Proportions and rendering: approximately five and a half head heights overall, small circular head, slender athletic line-like limbs, compact fists and minimal feet. Flat opaque charcoal-black artwork, white eyes only, smooth crisp antialiased edges. Remain unmistakably a stick figure; no anatomical musculature or broad human chest. No hair, clothing, gloves, armour, scarf or shoe details. No weapons, fire, particles, motion trails, aura, lighting tricks, gradients, shadows, text, labels or watermark. Coolness must come from the body mechanics and graphic silhouette. Center the full body with generous margins, genuinely transparent background with preserved alpha.
